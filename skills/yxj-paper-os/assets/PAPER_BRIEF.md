# Paper Brief

> A compact current state, not a chronology. Keep only information that cannot be
> recovered safely from the repository, evidence, manuscript, figures, feedback, or Git.
> Replace superseded content in place and never delay paper work to complete this file.
> This bundled asset is read-only during paper work. Use it only when a new fallback
> brief is needed and no repository format governs. Maintain existing briefs in their
> own format. Omit empty or recoverable entries; the prompts below are optional aids.

## Current basis

Record only a current basis or version choice that cannot be recovered reliably, with
its source locator. Include an adopted argument decision when it is not yet realized
in the manuscript. A new paper need not have a source or rendered artifact yet.

## Current constraints

Keep active unrecoverable scientific boundaries and adopted author directions, with
scope and source. Distinguish a requirement from a preference when that matters. Preserve
material adverse evidence that would otherwise be lost. Replace superseded entries;
identifiers or replacement links are needed only to resolve ambiguity. Repeated feedback
alone does not create a lasting rule.

## Open work

For each actual unfinished task, retain what remains, how completion will be recognized,
and any blocking input or decision. Record an affected edition or surface as deferred
only when the author explicitly excluded it from the current batch; include its dependency
and resumption condition. Unfinished in-scope work remains open. Later synchronization
must cover accumulated relevant changes before clearing a deferral.

An exploratory question is not an authorized revision. Remove completed work and its
temporary tracking; retain active directions. Read older tables and fields for their
current meaning without requiring a format conversion or treating an old mode as new
authorization. Use the project's existing history mechanism when available.
