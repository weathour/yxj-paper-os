## What makes an example complete

Match the example to the decision. A sentence needs enough preceding and following
context to explain its subject, comparison or connective. A paragraph case includes
the entire native paragraph, with equations and qualifications. An abstract case needs
the complete abstract. A transition case includes the units it connects. A section plan
names the sections, their distinct work and their actual relationships. A list of verbs
or a fill-in skeleton does not demonstrate organization.

Keep source text with its precise locator, analysis of that text, and any proposed
revision distinguishable. If full reproduction is inappropriate, retain a directly
readable native carrier and a complete-paragraph locator; do not reconstruct purported
published wording from a summary.

## Whole-paper architecture in practice

**Published organization.** Newbolt et al., *Nature Communications* 2024, distinguish average formation order, local restoring interaction and amplification along a chain. The [complete source analysis](/home/weathour/文档/yxj-wiki/refs/analyses/source-cards/newbolt2024flow-interactions-flight-formations.md#contradiction-to-validation) locates the Introduction, Results, Methods, Discussion and figures; it also records what was not independently verified. The following is a reading of that organization, not published wording.

The Introduction connects formation to a physical question. Results first define positions and normalized gaps, then use Fig. 2 to separate average order from a propagating, growing fluctuation. Fig. 3 gives controlled local disturbances and response. Fig. 4 introduces the wake model and compares distinct model outputs to the observations. Fig. 5 examines vacancy and phase changes; Discussion recovers what the combination says and its limits. Methods provide the apparatus, definitions and model needed to inspect those comparisons. Each part performs a different job in the same answer.

This design combines a problem-to-explanation sequence with nested comparisons. Within Fig. 2, multiple views of the same motion distinguish common displacement, relative position and normalized gaps; these views are related representations, not independent experiments. The model's multiple outputs correspond to the earlier dynamic, local-force and frequency-response questions. Its second stable branch helps motivate the vacancy configuration; Fig. 5 then provides evidence for specific changes rather than a universal intervention guarantee. Local response, chain propagation and the model retain their distinct objects and conditions.

**Alternative organization, proposed for comparison.** Given the same material, a different article could first compare observations of formation order and local restoring behavior, then collect the evidence on propagation, then develop the model and conditional interventions. This might favor a reader seeking an inventory of mechanisms, but it moves the reason for the local tests and the links between model outputs and particular observations. The change would need explicit cross-references, consistent measures and a later synthesis of how local and chain properties relate. It is a proposed reorganization, not a claim about an unpublished version or a superior design. The actual and proposed routes show how the main question changes grouping, placement, figure work and the explanatory burden.

## Selection: from completed work to an article's answer

**Published source and scope.** Newbolt et al., *Flow interactions lead to self-organized flight formations disrupted by self-amplifying waves*, Nature Communications 2024. Use the [complete source analysis](/home/weathour/文档/yxj-wiki/refs/analyses/source-cards/newbolt2024flow-interactions-flight-formations.md), [NC01-A](/home/weathour/文档/yxj-wiki/refs/analyses/english-prose/newbolt2024flow-interactions-flight-formations.md#nc01-a), and the actual Results/Figs.2–5 in the unchanged published carrier. This is an organization analysis of visible material, not evidence of what the authors privately chose to omit.

**Controlling tension.** Average spatial order and local restoring behavior coexist with amplified collective disturbances. Calling the whole system stable hides the problem; distinguishing these judgments makes the observation worth explaining. The wave diagnostics, local perturbations, model and changed configurations then have separate tasks in that explanation.

| Visible material | Job in the account | Consequence for selection and weight |
|---|---|---|
| Average spacing and time-varying gaps | Establish order and collective dynamics as distinct observations | Preserve both quantities and the normalization that permits interpretation. |
| Representative wave event and propagation features | Make the phenomenon traceable | Give the diagnostic connection explanatory space; a representative event is not population-frequency evidence. |
| Local perturbation and restoring response | Examine the local interaction | Keep its object distinct from the full chain's dynamic amplification. |
| Pair-based model and response comparison | Explain a possible propagation relationship | State the weak-damping/near-resonance conditions of the one-way account and what the model retains. |
| Vacancy or phase changes | Examine how an altered condition changes behavior | Preserve changes in feedback forcing or its magnitude, not just the favorable resulting trajectory. |

**Transfer.** Select the material that lets readers distinguish the coexistence, follow its explanation and judge the condition-dependent changes. Familiar setup can be compact; a short condition can be central. Give complementary support its actual role instead of placing every result in one performance ranking. Retained claims still need their own evidence and qualifications. A theoretical article whose principal answer is a difficult construction can require substantially more main-text derivation, as the next case shows.

## When the model and derivation carry the explanation

**Published case.** Trefethen, *Pseudospectra of Linear Operators*, SIAM Review 1997,
[TH02-B](/home/weathour/文档/yxj-wiki/refs/analyses/english-prose/trefethen1997pseudospectra-linear-operators.md#th02-b):
§5, Example 1, the complete second paragraph, printed p.389. Read the preceding
spectrum/growth distinction, the entire construction paragraph, and the following
pseudospectral abscissa and Theorem 1 in the linked publication PDF.

The paragraph turns a surprising long-time growth relation into a traceable object.
After crediting the example's source, it adds successive Jordan blocks, states the
infinite construction, compares the spectrum and pseudospectrum, then returns to the
growth interpretation. Repeated construction verbs preserve the rule; numerical choices
are distinguished from the structure needed for the unbounded operator. Moving the
construction out of view would remove this paragraph's explanation.

The next paragraph and theorem have a different job: they state a general relationship
under explicit operator and semigroup conditions. The example is an explanation, not
that proof. This selected case does not justify transferring an infinite-dimensional
long-time conclusion to a finite vehicle chain. Selection follows what must be understood
and checked, rather than treating all models, calculations or examples as appendix material.

## Review: a field map with explanatory depth

**Published source.** Merel, Botvinick and Wayne, *Hierarchical motor control in mammals and machines*, Nature Communications 2019, Perspective. Read the native HTML through [NC05-A/B](/home/weathour/文档/yxj-wiki/refs/analyses/english-prose/merel2019hierarchical-motor-control.md#nc05-a) and the source analysis. The article synthesizes design principles; it does not report a new experiment proving a universal hierarchy.

| Part of the published account | Concrete explanatory job |
|---|---|
| Introduction and cross-field setting | Locate the difficulty of generating flexible, diverse behavior and fairly recover the value of task optimization. |
| Core principles entry and Table 1 | Identify the authors' synthesis, target systems and route to detailed explanations. |
| Information factorization and task-conditioned discussion | Explain why the role of higher levels changes with action space, body, task, species and learning/execution conditions. |
| Concluding remarks | Recover the shared problem, retain existing gains, and form a research agenda rather than another inventory of principles. |

**Transfer.** A review can advance a question by explaining which principle addresses which difficulty and when their priority changes. It can retain complementary branches. Comparisons across biological and artificial systems supply a reasoned design vocabulary; they do not by themselves establish physical or causal equivalence. Use the complete original entry paragraph to see how source motivation, author synthesis, applicable domain and reading navigation remain distinct.

## Method: a design that answers an observed difficulty

**Published cases.** Blanchini et al., IEEE TAC 2010,
[TH01-A](/home/weathour/文档/yxj-wiki/refs/analyses/english-prose/blanchini2010stable-lpv-realization-parametric.md#th01-a)
(§I, p.2271, right column), and Yu & Yeo, TRC 2025,
[TR02-A](/home/weathour/文档/yxj-wiki/refs/analyses/english-prose/yu2025commercial-acc-dynamic-characteristics.md#tr02-a)
(§1.1.2, p.5).

TH01-A gives a realization task, an existence gap, and the resulting existence answer.
TR02-A instead preserves a measurement method's useful domain, defines its operation,
explains why event identification becomes difficult in another domain, and forms a
situation-specific method requirement. The former reports a mathematical answer;
the latter motivates a later design. Their concluding verbs and qualifications cannot
be exchanged merely to make both paragraphs sound decisive.

## Empirical: a claim tied to the experiment

**Published case.** MacLeod et al., Nature Communications 2022,
[NC03-A](/home/weathour/文档/yxj-wiki/refs/analyses/english-prose/macleod2022self-driving-laboratory-pareto.md#nc03-a),
“Quantification of algorithm performance,” its complete first paragraph. The ten-sentence
unit identifies the simulated response surface, noise scenarios, random-search baseline
and comparison metrics before reporting sample counts. It retains performance degradation
under noise beside the remaining advantage, then derives a bounded methodological use.
The response surface is fitted from experiments; the algorithm comparison is simulation,
not a record of equally many new physical experiments or measured industrial speed.

**Transfer.** Put the comparison and its evidence carrier where they govern the result.
Retain an adverse condition next to the favorable result, and locate the evidence carrier before making the quantitative comparison.

## Theory: distinguish achievable performance from limitations

**Published case.** Blanchini et al.,
[TH01-B](/home/weathour/文档/yxj-wiki/refs/analyses/english-prose/blanchini2010stable-lpv-realization-parametric.md#th01-b),
§VII, complete second paragraph, p.2280 right column. It preserves the available combination
of pointwise optimality and LPV stability, then identifies the time-varying performance
guarantee still missing. A related route is compared on that precise combination, and
the last sentence names an open realization problem. An unestablished guarantee is not
an impossibility theorem; replacing the knowledge boundary with an absolute negative
would change the result.

## Display: preserve relationships while changing appearance

**Published comparison.** MacLeod et al., Fig.4 and the algorithm-comparison section. Read the actual display and full caption through the [source analysis](/home/weathour/文档/yxj-wiki/refs/analyses/source-cards/macleod2022self-driving-laboratory-pareto.md) and [NC03-A](/home/weathour/文档/yxj-wiki/refs/analyses/english-prose/macleod2022self-driving-laboratory-pareto.md#nc03-a). The display compares search progress under defined noise scenarios on an experimentally fitted response surface. Hypervolume is evaluated at the corresponding noise-free points, separating information available to the search algorithm from the underlying performance used to score it.

**Transfer.** Settle this scientific distinction before choosing visual emphasis. Keep the information and evaluation objects recoverable, and retain noise-related degradation beside the remaining advantage. Changing colors, coordinate order or panel composition must preserve the distinction and baseline. Paper OS chooses the judgment, evidence scope and prominence; thesis-figure-skill produces and checks the concrete representation. This figure does not establish measured industrial runtime.

## Book-guided choices in published paragraphs

These routes connect the conditional [four-book methods](writing-books.md) to actual
published units. Read the linked complete originals and their analyses, not every case
for every task. Proposed prose remains the current author's writing; the published
paragraph is an observation of another article's choices, not a guarantee transferable
to the current manuscript. Select the unit whose actual job matches the decision.

### Question, theory plan, abstract and closing

C3–4/C8–11, W6–9/W18 and S7 help distinguish topic, question, answer and significance.
Compare [TH01-A/B](/home/weathour/文档/yxj-wiki/refs/analyses/english-prose/blanchini2010stable-lpv-realization-parametric.md#th01-a):
the introduction answers whether a stable realization exists; the conclusion separates
that answer from a stronger performance question. These are paragraph cases. An abstract
revision still needs its own complete abstract and scientific support, not a fabricated
abstract stitched from the two passages.

### Method: explain why each operation answers the question

C7–11 and W7–8 connect an operation to the object it must deliver.
[TH05-A](/home/weathour/文档/yxj-wiki/refs/analyses/english-prose/atoui2021interpolation-multi-lpv.md#th05-a)
connects a construction to its nonminimal state-space representation and scheduling
interface; [TH05-B](/home/weathour/文档/yxj-wiki/refs/analyses/english-prose/atoui2021interpolation-multi-lpv.md#th05-b)
then closes explicit proof conditions using their assumptions and variable choices.
Read the intervening theorem, block transformation and inequalities with the paragraphs.
The construction overview does not establish fast computation, and the proof closing
does not replace the earlier block argument.

### Empirical: retain the adverse result and comparison scope

T17, C8–9 and W8/18 require quantity, comparison and evidence scope.
[NC03-A](/home/weathour/文档/yxj-wiki/refs/analyses/english-prose/macleod2022self-driving-laboratory-pareto.md#nc03-a)
keeps noise-related degradation beside advantage over the specified baseline. Its numerical
comparison has a defined simulated object. Do not add a significance test, dispersion or
physical deployment result that the source or current study does not provide.

### Review: fair agreement without a manufactured dispute

C5–6, T2/4/15/18 and W6 organize sources by a real shared question.
[NC05-A/B](/home/weathour/文档/yxj-wiki/refs/analyses/english-prose/merel2019hierarchical-motor-control.md#nc05-a)
turn cross-field synthesis into principles, then retain the older optimization viewpoint's
value while criticizing overemphasis on isolated tasks. A complement or change in task
scope need not become a claim that all preceding work failed.

### Paragraph flow, passive voice and useful nominalization

S3–6, C17, W12–14 and T8 direct attention to objects, actions and continuity.
[NC02-A](/home/weathour/文档/yxj-wiki/refs/analyses/english-prose/dellarossa2020multilayer-symmetry.md#nc02-a)
introduces a representation before using it to explain a mapping; the passive maintains
perturbations as the object being tracked. In
[TH01-B](/home/weathour/文档/yxj-wiki/refs/analyses/english-prose/blanchini2010stable-lpv-realization-parametric.md#th01-b),
the available guarantee remains the topic. Technical nominalization can name that object;
automatically inserting an author subject would change the focus without improving it.

### Long sentence, punctuation and exact endpoints

S9–11/Appendix I and W12/16 ask whether the main relationship remains recoverable.
[TR01-B](/home/weathour/文档/yxj-wiki/refs/analyses/english-prose/montanino2021homogeneous-heterogeneous-traffic-flows.md#tr01-b)
uses a long first sentence with model and averaging restrictions, then a short second
sentence reverses the comparison for another quantity. Splitting is useful only if these
restrictions and the two quantities survive. Exact inequality endpoints belong to the
current scientific statement; this source is not an invented endpoint exercise.

### Display design: lookup and a claim-bearing caption

C15/T17/W8 select displays by the judgment they support.
[NC03-A](/home/weathour/文档/yxj-wiki/refs/analyses/english-prose/macleod2022self-driving-laboratory-pareto.md#nc03-a)
defines two comparison metrics and attaches each to a figure;
[NC01-A](/home/weathour/文档/yxj-wiki/refs/analyses/english-prose/newbolt2024flow-interactions-flight-formations.md#nc01-a)
explains which observed features support the physical classification. These selected
paragraphs are body-text interpretations, not complete-caption exemplars. Caption work
requires reading the native caption and display as its own complete unit.

### Source quotation: frame, explain and keep authority separate

T3, C11/14 and S Appendix II require attribution, preserved meaning and a reason for
using the source. [TH02-B](/home/weathour/文档/yxj-wiki/refs/analyses/english-prose/trefethen1997pseudospectra-linear-operators.md#th02-b)
credits the example's creator before explaining the construction; this is source attribution,
not a direct-quotation case. Exact book wording about a warrant is available through
[the local book reader](writing-books.md#exact-local-source-lookup).

### Material objection: resolve the specific gap

C10–11, T6 and W18 help distinguish a missing result, a missing inferential connection
and an unwritten explanation. Identify the needed research or adjust an unsupported
claim when the result is missing; repair the argument or explanation when the result
is already established. Keep other established claims and the paper's object at their
supported scope.
[NC02-B](/home/weathour/文档/yxj-wiki/refs/analyses/english-prose/dellarossa2020multilayer-symmetry.md#nc02-b)
retains experimental agreement under small nonidealities while admitting that a quantitative
tolerance boundary is not established. [TH01-B](/home/weathour/文档/yxj-wiki/refs/analyses/english-prose/blanchini2010stable-lpv-realization-parametric.md#th01-b)
retains stability while naming the performance question. A limitation sentence cannot
supply a missing guarantee, and experimental tolerance is not a general robustness proof.

## Transfer the work, not the template

Recover what readers need to judge, the scientific relations, and the evidence and
explanation needed before choosing wording. A field map, construction rationale,
experimental comparison, proof sequence and result-led article call for different development.

Use another original when the required organizational job is absent. Read the complete
unit with its neighbors and explain the choice under the current paper's own conditions.
Retain effective prose and keep a terminology correction focused on the affected use.
